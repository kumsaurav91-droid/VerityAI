      Everything you need to know about VerityAI

      How does VerityAI detect fake career advice?

      VerityAI uses Google's advanced Gemini AI to analyze content for red flags like unrealistic timelines, extreme claims, lack of evidence, toxic productivity culture, and AI-generated slop. It evaluates credibility, realism, sustainability, and potential harm to provide a comprehensive assessment.

      What is 'AI Slop' in career advice?

      'AI Slop' refers to low-quality, AI-generated content that lacks original insight, uses generic templates, contains contradictory information, or is filled with buzzwords without substance. It's content created quickly by AI without human verification or expertise, often misleading readers with surface-level advice.

      Is VerityAI free to use?

      Yes! VerityAI is 100% free to use. No account required, no hidden costs, and no subscriptions. We built this tool to help students make better decisions about the advice they consume online. Your content is never stored, and we don't track your usage.

      How accurate is the Truth Score?

      Our AI analysis has an 85%+ accuracy rate based on testing. The confidence score shows how certain the AI is about its assessment. While highly accurate, we always recommend using your own critical thinking alongside VerityAI's analysis. No AI is perfect, and your judgment matters!

      Can I use VerityAI for LinkedIn posts?

      Absolutely! VerityAI works great for analyzing LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, blog articles, course descriptions, or any text-based career and productivity advice. Just copy and paste the content, and our AI will analyze it for you. Perfect for evaluating influencer advice before following it!
