About VerityAI

Why VerityAI exists and what it will and will not do.

Why it exists

Students keep seeing promises like "100% placement", "50 LPA in 3 months" and "pay a small fee to confirm your offer". Many are exaggerated, and some are scams. VerityAI gives students a quick second opinion before they spend money, time or health on them.

How we try to be fair

  • We show the reasons behind every verdict, with the exact phrases that raised concern.
  • We separate the risk score from evidence confidence, and we say clearly when a claim could not be verified.
  • We label every feature as available or planned, and we do not show results we did not produce.

What it will not do

VerityAI cannot confirm that a company or person is real, and it is not legal, financial or career counselling. The AI can be wrong, so use it as a second opinion and always check the official source.

Who built it

VerityAI is an open-source project by Saurav Kumar. The code is on GitHub, and you can send feedback or report problems there.
