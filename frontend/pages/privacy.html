Privacy Policy

What VerityAI sends, stores and shares when you use it.

What you submit

When you run a check, the text, link, screenshot, PDF or YouTube link you submit is sent to this app's server and then to Google's Gemini API for analysis. The server does not keep your submission after the result is returned. Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times.

Other services involved

  • Links: the server fetches the page you link to so it can be analysed.
  • Sender and link check: domain names found in your text are looked up in a public registration database (RDAP).
  • Claim check: when you ask for it, Gemini uses Google Search to look for sources.
  • YouTube: the video title is requested from YouTube, and the video is read by Gemini.
  • Fonts: your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts when you visit.

What stays on your device

Your history and your light or dark theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage, and the app may cache its own files to load faster. Nothing in your history is uploaded. You can delete one result or all of them on the History page, or clear your browser's site data.

Feedback

If you send feedback, we save your rating, the type of check, the verdict, the score and the optional comment you wrote. We do not save the text you checked.

What we do not do

VerityAI has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics trackers.

Your choices and contact

Please do not submit other people's sensitive personal information. For questions or removal requests, open an issue on GitHub.
